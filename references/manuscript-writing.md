## Language Default

Draft Korean manuscripts by default. Preserve statistical notation, variable
names, software names, dataset names, and citations accurately.

## Methods and Results

For Methods:

- use the source guide and analysis log;
- describe data source, study design, participants, operational definitions,
  derived-variable rules, statistical methods, software, and design-specific
  details such as survey weights or matching.

For Results:

- report findings before interpretation;
- match the order of tables and research questions;
- include statistics needed for the method and journal context;
- keep coding direction clear.

## Introduction and Discussion

Use results to shape the argument without reverse-engineering unsupported
claims:

1. extract the focal findings;
2. identify literature needed for burden, gap, comparator results, and
   implications;
3. verify literature before citing it;
4. build an Introduction with problem, gap, need, and purpose;
5. build a Discussion with result summary, comparison, explanation,
   occupational-therapy or health-science implications, limitations, and future
   work.

## Korean Prose

Prefer natural academic Korean:

- use precise verbs instead of inflated nominal phrases;
- avoid repeating generic "본 연구는" openings when a clearer subject exists;
- avoid turning association results into causal claims;
- keep Results factual and Discussion interpretive.

Example Results pattern:

```text
로지스틱 회귀분석 결과, [예측변수]는 [결과사건]과 유의한 관련성을 보였다
(OR = [값], 95% CI [하한]-[상한], p = [값]).
```

Example limitation pattern:

```text
본 연구는 2차자료를 이용한 관찰연구이므로 변수 간 시간적 선후관계와
인과적 기전을 단정하는 데 제한이 있다.
```
